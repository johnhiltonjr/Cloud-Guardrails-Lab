# ADR-001: Assign the Security Baseline Initiative at Management-Group Scope

- Status: Proposed
- Date: 2026-11-23
- Author: John Hilton Jr.
- Module: 01 — Guardrails as Code
- Related controls: C-01, C-02, C-03, C-04, C-05

## Context

Harbor and Pine Insurance lacks a consistently enforced Azure security
baseline. The seeded environment demonstrates gaps in resource ownership,
approved deployment locations, storage security, and Key Vault network access.

The organization needs a common baseline across its lab subscriptions,
while allowing documented exceptions for justified business requirements.

## Decision Drivers

- Security: Apply consistent security requirements across environments.
- Operational impact: Introduce enforcement gradually to identify workload
  dependencies and avoid unnecessary deployment disruption.
- Governance: Require documented, approved, and time-limited exceptions.
- Cost and complexity: Reduce duplicated assignments and configuration drift
  across subscriptions.
- Lab constraints: Keep assignments within the isolated lab hierarchy and
  preserve existing seeded violations for Module 2 remediation.

## Options Considered

| Option | Benefits | Drawbacks |
|---|---|---|
| A: Assign the initiative at management-group scope | Child subscriptions inherit a common baseline; fewer assignments to maintain; centralized oversight | Assignment errors have a broader impact; exception handling can become a bottleneck |
| B: Assign the initiative separately to each subscription | Allows independent rollout and subscription-specific configuration | Duplicated administration; greater risk of inconsistent assignments and configuration drift |

## Decision

Assign the security baseline initiative at `mg-guardrails-lab`,
using Terraform to manage the initiative and assignment.

Begin with Audit effects to establish compliance visibility.
Move C-02, storage public-network access, to Deny after validation.

Other controls remain in their documented initial effects until a
separate enforcement review approves changes.

### Scope

The assignment applies to subscriptions and supported resources beneath
`mg-guardrails-lab`, according to each policy's applicability.

In-scope subscriptions:
- `sub-hp-lab-nonprod-01`, `sub-hp-lab-prod-01` and `sub-hp-lab-platform-01` 

Any exclusions or exemptions must be explicitly documented.

## Rationale

Management-group assignment provides a common inherited baseline and
reduces the need to maintain separate assignments for each subscription.

Subscription-level assignments were not selected because independently
maintained assignments increase the likelihood of inconsistent coverage.

Starting in Audit provides evidence of existing violations and allows
dependencies to be assessed before introducing deployment restrictions.

Central assignment supports consistent governance. CI checks provide
earlier feedback to developers before deployment.

## Consequences

### Benefits

- Consistent baseline coverage across in-scope subscriptions.
- Central visibility into compliance findings.
- Reduced duplication of policy assignments.
- Prevention of new C-02 violations after Deny is enabled.

### Risks and Limitations

- Incorrect assignment changes can affect multiple subscriptions.
- Centralized exception handling may delay legitimate deployments.
- Audit identifies violations but does not prevent them.
- Deny does not automatically remediate existing noncompliant resources.
- These controls address specific configurations and do not establish
  complete workload security.

### Operational Responsibilities

- Security maintains policy configuration, initiative membership,
  assignments, and validation evidence through Terraform.
- Workload owners assess application impact and remediate their resources.
- The risk committee approves exception requests and residual-risk acceptance.
- Security implements approved exemptions with an owner, justification,
  expiration date, and review requirements.

## Validation

| Test | Expected Result | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Inspect the management-group assignment and child subscription coverage | The initiative is assigned at the intended scope and inherited by in-scope subscriptions | [Assignment export/link] |
| Evaluate seeded resources in Audit | Expected violations are reported against the corresponding controls | [Compliance export/link] |
| Evaluate the compliant reference resource | The resource passes applicable baseline controls | [Compliance result/link] |
| Enable C-02 Deny and attempt a new deployment with storage public-network access enabled | Azure rejects the deployment with RequestDisallowedByPolicy referencing C-02 | [Deployment error/link] |
| Deploy a storage account that satisfies applicable controls | Azure permits the deployment | [Deployment result/link] |

Before enabling C-02 Deny, confirm that:
- Audit results match the expected resource configurations.
- Affected workload dependencies have been reviewed.
- Required exceptions have been approved and documented.
- Compliant and noncompliant deployment tests are ready.

## Rollback and Review

If C-02 Deny causes an unexpected legitimate deployment failure,
assess whether a scoped exemption or an enforcement rollback is appropriate.

For rollback, change C-02 from Deny to Audit through Terraform,
review and apply the change, and verify the effective assignment.
Record the reason and resulting temporary exposure.

Review this decision when:
- Subscription placement or the management-group hierarchy changes.
- Policy changes produce unexpected deployment failures.
- Repeated exceptions indicate that the baseline needs adjustment.
- Additional controls are proposed for Deny enforcement.
